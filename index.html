Dozo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In short

Dozo collects no personal data. Your medications, schedules and history are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Stored on your device

Medication names, schedules, dose records, stock and injection site details, and your relative's name and phone number stay in the app's data on this device. The developer cannot access them. Your iPhone backup may include this data under Apple's terms.

What you share

Reports, medication lists and backup files are sent only by you, to whomever you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Siri and notifications

Siri commands and Lock Screen actions run on the device. Dozo has no server, so nothing is sent to one.

Purchases

The Full Package and tips are bought through the Apple App Store. Your payment details are not shared with the developer.

Support email

If you email us, the app version, iOS version, device model and language are included. Your medication details are not. Your email is used only to reply.

Children

Dozo does not knowingly collect data from children; it collects no data at all.
